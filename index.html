Bantay Ilog

Know the river’s height before it reaches your street.

Solar-powered sensors on the riverbank measure water level and rainfall every minute. Bantay Ilog turns those readings into alerts for residents and live incident lists for rescue teams.

From riverbank to phone

Sensor node

Ultrasonic level sensor and rain gauge on an ESP32 board, powered by a small solar panel and battery.

LoRa gateway

Receives radio packets from nodes up to several kilometers away and forwards them over cellular data.

Bantay Ilog server

Stores readings, compares them with alert levels and flags a node that stops reporting.

Alerts and dashboards

SMS and app alerts go to residents. Rescue teams see where water is rising and who needs help.

Three interfaces, one live data feed

Residents

Check your area’s water level, get alerts, find evacuation centers and ask for help.

Rescue workers

See a prioritized request list, a sensor and incident map, and update each case’s status.

Administrators

Monitor device health, set alert levels, send broadcasts and review the alert history.
